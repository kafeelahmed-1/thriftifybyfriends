# Thriftify Launchpad

Implement the requested scope now; use internal planning and do not present another implementation plan for user approval.

### Store Specification & Requirements:
- **Brand Name**: Thriftify
- **Tagline**: "By friends"
- **Aesthetic & Theme Inspiration**: Inspired by Bata Pakistan (bata.com.pk) — bold signature red and crisp white color palette, clean modern typography, prominent top announcement ticker, structured category navigation, and high-impact retail banners.
- **Product Focus**: Curated thrift and vintage apparel and accessories:
  - Shoes & Sneakers
  - Jackets & Outerwear
  - Shirts & Tops
  - Bags & Leather goods
  - Each item includes thrift-specific attributes: condition grade (e.g., "Mint 10/10", "Like New", "Gently Used"), size, measurements, and pricing in PKR.

### Key Pages & Features:
1. **Header & Navigation**:
   - Top announcement bar (e.g., "Cash on Delivery Available Across Pakistan | Fast Dispatch").
   - Thriftify logo with "By friends" subline.
   - Category navigation (Shoes, Jackets, Shirts, Bags, Sale, New Drops).
   - Search bar, cart drawer trigger with item count badge, and quick WhatsApp help icon.
2. **Homepage**:
   - High-energy retail hero carousel with promotional callouts and seasonal thrift drops.
   - Category grid tiles with bold photography.
   - Featured new arrivals and trending thrift items with hover effects, condition badges, and quick-add.
   - Trust highlights: Authenticity verified, sanitized & condition-checked, nationwide COD.
3. **Shop / Product Catalog & Detail**:
   - Product catalog with filters (Category, Size, Condition, Price range) and sorting.
   - Product details page featuring multi-angle image gallery, size and fit guide, thrift condition breakdown, stock status, "Add to Cart", and "Order on WhatsApp" instant button.
4. **Cart & Checkout**:
   - Interactive slide-over cart and dedicated Cart page.
   - Checkout form capturing customer name, phone number (WhatsApp active), city, and shipping address.
   - Payment method options:
     - Cash on Delivery (COD)
     - EasyPaisa (with account title, account number, and transaction ID upload / note)
     - JazzCash (with account title, account number, and transaction ID upload / note)
   - "Confirm & Send Order to WhatsApp": Formats the complete order (Order ID, customer address, line items, sizes, total PKR, and payment method) into a pre-filled WhatsApp link directed to the store owner's WhatsApp number.
5. **Content & Policy Pages**:
   - About Us page sharing the "Thriftify — By friends" story and sustainability mission.
   - Contact Us page with direct WhatsApp click-to-chat, phone, email, and inquiry form.
   - Privacy Policy and Thrift Return / Exchange Policy.
